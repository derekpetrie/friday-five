AI in Marketing
5 Bullet Friday
September 18, 2026

Two major platforms bet their roadmaps on autonomous agents this week — while new data says your brand and your people still outweigh any tool you buy.

1
HubSpot Rebuilds Around AI Agents
  • A new Campaign Agent takes a goal and builds the full campaign — media plan, copy, schedule — no manual setup required.
  • The "Breeze" AI brand is dead; named agents now coordinate across marketing, sales, and ads including ChatGPT Ads.
  • If you're on HubSpot, "using the platform" is about to mean telling an agent what you want, not clicking through workflows.

2
Answer Engine Optimization Is Now a Unicorn
  • Profound raised $180M at a $1.8B valuation to help brands appear in AI-generated answers — Sequoia and Kleiner led.
  • Over 1,000 enterprise brands now pay to surface in ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity results, not just Google.
  • If you haven't budgeted for AEO alongside SEO, you're already behind the enterprises writing the playbook.

3
Brand Still Beats the Algorithm
  • Bazaarvoice surveyed 3,600+ consumers: 65% click the brand they recognize, just 24% take the AI's top pick.
  • Unknown brands need a 50% discount or overwhelming review proof before shoppers switch from a name they trust.
  • AI shopping agents are reinforcing brand incumbency, not disrupting it — don't trade brand-building budget for GEO spend.
Bazaarvoice via GlobeNewswire
4
Talent Now Rivals Tech as Growth Driver
  • Deloitte surveyed 300+ VP-and-above marketing leaders: an equal share now cite "right people" over "right tech" for growth.
  • Some CMOs reported reverting to proven strategies under C-suite pressure, pushing back on tool-first AI adoption.
  • Your 2027 budget case needs a talent line, not just a tools line — the people gap now matches the tech gap.
MarketScale / Deloitte
5
Twelve States Now Regulate AI Marketing
  • Colorado now requires consumer notice and human review after automated adverse decisions; Connecticut targets employment AI.
  • California mandates public disclosure of AI training data sources — directly affecting content-generation tools you may use.
  • If your personalization engine makes automated decisions about what customers see, you need a state-by-state compliance map now.
