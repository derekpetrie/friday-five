AI in Marketing
5 Bullet Friday
October 2, 2026

Nearly everyone adopted AI. Almost nobody can prove it's working. This week made the gap hard to ignore — and showed what the few who've crossed it actually did differently.

1
Most AI Marketing Isn't Working Yet
  • Bain surveyed 1,397 CMOs: only 6% say AI delivers significant performance impact, despite near-universal adoption.
  • Leaders are 3.7x more likely to fully redesign workflows and 8.5x more likely to run 100+ experiments a month.
  • If your AI play is "same work, faster," you're in the 94%. Redesign the workflow first.
Bain & Company
2
Autonomous Agents Run B2B Outbound
  • Clay and Marketbridge are piloting AI agents that research, prioritize, and engage accounts across 200+ data sources at LG Electronics.
  • The pilot cut lead response from one to two weeks down to one business day — without continuous human direction.
  • If a Fortune 500 trusts agents for live outbound, your "should we pilot?" conversation is already late.
GlobeNewswire
3
First-Party Targeting Enters ChatGPT Ads
  • LiveRamp expanded RampID to ChatGPT's ad inventory, letting brands push deterministic audience segments into conversational AI.
  • Same identity-based targeting you use in display and social, now available in the chat window.
  • A paid discovery channel is forming inside AI chat. Budget for test spend in Q1.
Martech.org
4
California AI Ad Disclosure Starts January
  • SB 1050 requires conspicuous disclosure when ads feature AI-generated performers — effective January 1, 2027.
  • Companion bills tighten data broker deletion to 30 days and expand consumer rights over third-party data.
  • If your creative team uses AI-generated people in campaigns, start your compliance audit now.
Venable LLP
5
AI Made Marketing Faster, Not Smarter
  • Only 13% of marketing teams can measure any AI impact — the same number as 2025, despite tripled AI training.
  • Marketing's ownership of AI strategy fell from 48% to 20% in one year as IT took the wheel.
  • Speed without measurement is just faster guessing. Fight to keep AI strategy in marketing's hands.
Fluvio Research
