The shiny-tool era is over — this week brought disclosure mandates, measurement standards, and hard questions about who's really running the models under your stack.

EU Disclosure Rules Now Apply to Your Ads
  • Article 50 went live August 2 — AI chatbots, generated ads, and synthetic media reaching EU audiences now require disclosure labels.
  • Penalties reach €15M or 3% of global revenue, and the liability lands on your marketing team, not the AI vendor.
  • Audit every AI content workflow touching Europe before your legal team does it for you.

IAB Creates AI Search Visibility Framework
  • First shared vocabulary for AI search: presence, prominence, portrayal, and persuasion — the "4 P's of AI Visibility."
  • Only 16% of brands track whether AI platforms mention them; the rest are flying blind on a fast-growing channel.
  • If you're not measuring share of model alongside share of voice, your competitive intelligence has a blind spot.

3
HubSpot Ships Feature That Sparked Data Revolt
  • Contact Discovery went live August 4 after a July firestorm forced HubSpot to reverse its default-opt-in data-pooling terms.
  • Original terms pooled customer data across HubSpot's base without explicit consent; backlash killed them in four days.
  • Every SaaS renewal this year will test the line between "AI feature" and "training on your data" — read the terms.

4
AI Slop Becomes a Brand Safety Metric
  • DoubleVerify surveyed 22,000 consumers: 42% say ads next to low-quality AI content would hurt their view of a brand.
  • 53% of marketers already worry about AI adjacency; DV launched a detection product to filter it in media buys.
  • Add a quality floor for AI-generated environments to your programmatic criteria — the usual blocklist isn't enough anymore.

5
Chinese Models Quietly Run Your AI Stack
  • US companies route nearly a third of AI tokens through Chinese open-weight models — at one-tenth the cost of US alternatives.
  • Most marketing teams don't know which model their vendors actually use; the savings are real but so are the compliance gaps.
  • Ask your AI vendors one question this week: which models process our data, and where?
