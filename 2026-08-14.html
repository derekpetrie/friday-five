AI in Marketing
5 Bullet Friday
August 14, 2026

Everyone's using AI now. This week's question is whether anyone's using it well — and what happens when the rules change mid-sprint.

1
Nielsen Buys DoubleVerify for $2.15B
  • All-cash deal merges audience measurement with ad verification into a single independent platform.
  • Combined entity covers $240B in ad spend across TV, CTV, social, and mobile.
  • If you run separate measurement and verification vendors, that two-vendor model just got an expiration date.
Marketing Dive
2
Agencies Optimize AI for the Wrong Thing
  • Forrester finds 90% of agencies use generative AI, but for 81% the top goal is staff productivity — not creative quality.
  • The industry is "mistaking efficiency for effectiveness," trading long-term brand growth for short-term savings.
  • At your next agency review, ask what they're reinvesting AI savings into — talent and ambition, or just margins.
BizTechReports / Forrester
3
AI Is Your Buyer's First Gatekeeper Now
  • Half of buyers now start product research with an AI agent, not a search engine or your website.
  • Vendor shortlists are shrinking as AI pre-filters suppliers before a human books a single call.
  • Test whether your brand surfaces when someone asks ChatGPT or Google AI Mode to solve what you sell.
MarketScale
4
AI Emails Match Humans — for Far Less
  • Chicago Booth tested AI vs. human-written emails across 27,500 customers — purchase rates were identical.
  • AI emails were shorter, produced faster, and drove the highest projected annual profit across all three trials.
  • Stop asking "is AI copy better?" and start asking "what do we do with the savings when it's just as good?"
Chicago Booth Review
5
EU AI Act Labeling Rules Are Live
  • As of August 2, all AI-generated marketing content served in the EU must carry machine-readable disclosure.
  • Chatbots must identify as AI, deepfakes must be labeled, and fines reach €15M or 3% of global revenue.
  • Audit your AI content pipeline now — these rules apply regardless of where your company is headquartered.
