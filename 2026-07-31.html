AI in Marketing
5 Bullet Friday
July 31, 2026

AI adoption is effectively universal. This week: growing evidence that having it and getting value from it are two very different things.

1
ChatGPT Opens Self-Serve Ads to All
  • OpenAI killed the $50K minimum — any US advertiser can now buy ChatGPT placements, with Best Buy and Lowe's already in.
  • Targeting runs on conversation context, not keywords — a fundamentally different creative brief.
  • The channel hit $100M in six weeks. Test a small budget now or explain later why you didn't.
AI Weekly
2
Everyone Has AI, Few See Results
  • CMI data: 95% of B2B marketers use AI, but fewer than 40% report meaningful performance gains.
  • Where it does work: SDR and content-distribution workflows cut cost-per-lead 38%. Broad content generation still underperforms.
  • Stop spreading AI across everything — double down where your own data shows ROI.
MarketScale
3
AI Everywhere, Deadlines Nowhere
  • Knak surveyed 300+ enterprise teams: 70% run AI in production, yet 85% missed a campaign launch last year.
  • The bottleneck isn't the AI — it's approvals (47%), design (38%), and coordination (36%).
  • Before buying another AI tool, fix the human workflow it feeds into.
Knak via PR Newswire
4
Buyers Find You Faster, Close Slower
  • G2 data: 82% of B2B buyers use AI chatbots for vendor recs, but only 9% would let AI execute a purchase.
  • CFO veto power on approved deals jumped from 31% to 46% in one year; IT security review is the top delay.
  • Your top-of-funnel is faster, but finance and security own the timeline now — staff accordingly.
G2
5
Consumers Use AI More, Trust It Less
  • IAB research: 40% of consumers use AI daily, but only 2% trust the output without checking first.
  • Publisher click-through rates dropped to 45%, and 38% say AI has degraded their attribution.
  • If your brand uses AI visibly in CX, test whether customers want that — don't just assume they do.
